How this map is built

Sources

  • Crustdata company database: semantic searches across 16 climate sub-sectors, plus searches limited to companies with a funding round, grant or accelerator programme on record
  • Climate investor portfolios: Rainmatter, Avaana, Climate Angels, Sangam, Social Alpha, Omnivore
  • A researched list built from Inc42, Tracxn reports, VC portfolios and news, covering funding, founders and hiring signals

Who counts as a startup

  • Funded or VC-backed: a recorded round from pre-seed to late stage, a grant, accelerator or incubator support, or a known climate investor on the cap table
  • Incubated or recognised: the profile mentions an incubator, accelerator, DPIIT recognition, a grant or an award
  • Other startups: operating companies with a climate product that pass every filter below but have no funding signal yet

How the two maps work

Similarity: each startup's description is turned into a text vector (TF-IDF and SVD). UMAP places similar vectors close together, so startups that build similar things cluster. Colour shows the sector, and bigger dots mean larger teams or confirmed funding. "Most similar startups" are the 10 nearest neighbours by text.

India: each startup is pinned to its headquarters city using the GeoNames gazetteer, or to its state when only the state is known. Dots in the same city are fanned out so they don't overlap. Click a state to highlight its startups. Boundaries come from DataMeet (CC BY 2.5 India).

Coverage is strongest for funded startups. Data as of November 2026.
