About the data

Sources

  • Crustdata company database: semantic searches across 16 climate sub-sectors, plus searches restricted to companies with a funding round, grant or accelerator programme on record
  • Climate investor portfolios: Rainmatter, Avaana, Climate Angels, Sangam, Social Alpha
  • Researched list built from Inc42, Tracxn reports, VC portfolios and news (funding, founders, hiring signals)

Noise floor: who counts as a startup

  • Funded / VC-backed: a recorded round (pre-seed to late stage), grant, accelerator or incubator support ("non-equity assistance"), or a known climate investor in its cap table
  • Incubated / recognised: profile mentions an incubator, accelerator, DPIIT recognition, a grant or an award
  • Other startups: operating companies with a climate product that pass every filter below but have no funding signal yet

How the map works

Similarity map: each description becomes a text vector (TF-IDF + SVD). UMAP places similar vectors close together, so companies doing similar things cluster. Colours show the sub-sector; bigger dots mean larger teams or confirmed funding. "Most similar startups" are the 10 nearest neighbours by text similarity.

India map: each startup is pinned to its headquarters city (GeoNames gazetteer), or to its state when only the state is known. Dots in a city are fanned out so they don't overlap. Click a state to highlight its startups. Boundaries: DataMeet (CC BY 2.5 India).
